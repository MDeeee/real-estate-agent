## Location

Laurel Hills is a low-density gated community in the Golden Square area of New Cairo, close to the Ring Road interchange and the Regional Ring Road. Mall of Arabia is a short drive away for shopping, and several international schools sit within fifteen minutes.

## The villa

A standalone villa on a 650 m² corner plot, with 420 m² of built area over three floors. Four bedrooms including a ground-floor guest suite, a staff room with its own entrance, a double-height reception and a family lounge on the first floor. The roof terrace has an open view over the community park.

## Finishing and amenities

Fully finished with imported wood doors, a modern fitted kitchen, a private garden and space for a swimming pool. Community amenities include a clubhouse, tennis courts, walking and cycling tracks and a commercial strip with a supermarket and a pharmacy. Two covered parking spaces and one visitor space are included.
