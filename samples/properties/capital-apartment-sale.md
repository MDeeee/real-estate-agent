## Location

Skyline Residence is in the R7 residential district of the New Administrative Capital, a short drive from the Central Business District, the Iconic Tower and the Green River park. The electric train station and the Regional Ring Road link the Capital to Cairo, and the government district is only minutes away.

## The unit

A 135 m² apartment on the fifth floor with two bedrooms, two bathrooms and a large reception opening to a balcony with views over the green spine of the compound. The layout is rectangular with no wasted corridors, and every room has an external window.

## Finishing and amenities

Delivered semi finished with plastered walls, external windows installed and utilities connected to the unit. The compound includes a podium garden, a clubhouse, a swimming pool, a gym, retail units on the ground level and underground parking with one space per apartment. Security is provided by a staffed gate and CCTV.

## Payment plan

Ten percent down payment and instalments over eight years. Early-payment discounts are available on the final instalments.
