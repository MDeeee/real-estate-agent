## Location

Cedar Gardens is a family-oriented compound in Sheikh Zayed City, reached directly from the Cairo-Alexandria Desert Road. Arkan Plaza, Galleria 40 and Americana Plaza are all within a few minutes, and Sphinx International Airport is about thirty minutes away. Well-known schools and a major hospital are nearby.

## The townhouse

A middle townhouse of 280 m² on a 190 m² plot, with a private garden at the back and a car port at the front. Four bedrooms over two floors plus a roof room that can serve as a study or a guest room. The ground floor has an open-plan living and dining area with direct garden access.

## Finishing and amenities

Delivered semi finished: plastered walls and ceilings, external joinery and plumbing risers, leaving the owner free to choose floors and fittings. The compound offers a clubhouse, two swimming pools, a nursery, a sports court and landscaped green belts, with controlled gates at all entrances.

## Payment plan

Ten percent down payment and instalments over eight years. Resale units in the same phase are also available.

## Notes

A quiet cul-de-sac location with no facing neighbours at the back.
