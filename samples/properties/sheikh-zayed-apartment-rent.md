## Location

The apartment is on a quiet street in the Mohandessin Extension district of Sheikh Zayed, a ten-minute walk to Galleria 40 and Americana Plaza. The Sheikh Zayed Road and the Cairo-Alexandria Desert Road are both close, so the commute to Smart Village is short.

## The unit

A 130 m² apartment on the fourth floor of a modern building with a lift. Two bedrooms with built-in wardrobes, two bathrooms, a spacious reception with a balcony facing the street garden, and a fitted kitchen with a hob, hood and oven. Unfurnished, with air-conditioning units already installed in the bedrooms and the reception.
