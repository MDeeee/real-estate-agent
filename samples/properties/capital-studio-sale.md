## Location

Metro Lofts is a mid-rise development in the R8 district of the New Administrative Capital, close to the central park, the Oriental Mall area and a planned monorail stop. The Ministry district and the university campuses are within a short drive, which supports steady demand from staff and students.

## The unit

A 55 m² studio on the sixth floor with one bathroom and an open kitchen. The ceiling height is 3.2 metres, which leaves room for a mezzanine sleeping platform, and a full-width window lets in a lot of natural light. A small balcony faces the interior garden.

## Finishing and amenities

Delivered fully finished with a fitted kitchen and air-conditioning, ready to be furnished or rented. Residents have access to a gym, a co-working lounge, a rooftop garden, a convenience store and a laundry service. Security is round the clock and there is one parking space in the basement.

## Payment plan

A five percent down payment and instalments over ten years keep the monthly commitment low, which suits first-time investors.

## Notes

The developer offers a managed-rental programme that handles tenants and maintenance for a fixed percentage of the rent.
