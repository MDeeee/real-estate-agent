## Location

The apartment is on the eighth floor of a classic Zamalek building overlooking the Nile on the Gezira side. The Cairo Opera House, the Cairo Tower and the Gezira Sporting Club are all within a few minutes on foot, and Tahrir Square and downtown are a short drive across the bridge. Cafes, galleries, embassies and good restaurants line the surrounding streets.

## The unit

A 220 m² apartment with three bedrooms, three bathrooms and a long reception whose tall windows open onto a continuous balcony facing the river. The building dates from the 1950s and the unit has kept its high ceilings and original wooden doors, while the kitchen and bathrooms have been rebuilt to a modern standard.

## Finishing and amenities

Hardwood flooring, new wiring and plumbing, split air-conditioning and elegant built-in joinery. The building has two lifts, a doorman, a garage place on request and a roof garden. A grocery, a pharmacy and a bank branch are within a block.

## Payment

A cash transaction with a ten percent reservation deposit and final transfer within forty-five days.
