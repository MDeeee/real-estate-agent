## Location

Olive Court is a small residential complex a five-minute walk from the main gate of the American University in Cairo, which makes this studio popular with visiting lecturers, graduate students and young professionals. Restaurants, cafes and a supermarket are within walking distance, and the Ninety Street is two minutes by car.

## The unit

A bright 58 m² open-plan studio on the third floor with a lift. The sleeping area is separated from the living space by a sliding partition. Fully furnished with a queen bed, a sofa, a dining table for four, a desk, a TV and an equipped kitchenette with fridge, oven and washing machine.

## Amenities

High-speed fibre internet is already installed, with the bill included in the rent. Air conditioning in the main room, a water heater, 24-hour security, a rooftop terrace and one parking space in the courtyard.

## Rental terms

Monthly rent of 22,000 EGP with a two-month deposit. Minimum lease is six months, and a twelve-month lease earns a reduced rate. Utilities are billed separately except internet.

## Notes

Non-smoking building. Small pets are considered case by case.
