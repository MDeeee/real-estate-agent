## Location

White Sands is a quiet beachfront community in Sidi Abdel Rahman, about two hours from Cairo and fifteen minutes from the Alamein International Airport. The village sits on a long, sheltered sandy beach, with the Alamein Boulevard, restaurants and the Marassi marina area close by.

## The villa

A five-bedroom villa of 380 m² set directly on the sand, with a large covered terrace, a private pool and a garden that opens onto the beach. The master suite and two further bedrooms have sea views and balconies. A separate staff wing is included, along with a family lounge and a fully equipped kitchen with a barbecue area.

## Furnishing and services

Fully furnished in a light coastal style with air conditioning in every room, satellite TV, high-speed internet and a backup generator. Weekly housekeeping and pool maintenance are included in the rent. The community provides beach lifeguards, 24-hour security and a shuttle to the village centre.

## Rental terms

Monthly rent of 150,000 EGP during the season, from June to September, with a minimum rental of three months and a 30 percent deposit on booking. Off-season rates are available on request.

## Notes

Pets are not allowed. Sleeps up to twelve people comfortably.
