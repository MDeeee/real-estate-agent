## Location

Sunset Valley is a gated community in the Dreamland area of 6th of October City, off the Wahat Road. Dreamland Golf and the Hosary Mosque district are close, and the Grand Egyptian Museum and the Pyramids can be reached in about twenty-five minutes. Schools and a hospital sit within the larger Dreamland catchment.

## The villa

A twin villa of 330 m² on a 450 m² plot, sharing one wall with its neighbour. Five bedrooms over two floors, a ground-floor reception with a double-height ceiling, a separate family room and a roof terrace. The private garden has a pre-approved plan for a 6 by 12 metre swimming pool.

## Finishing and amenities

Delivered as core and shell, giving the buyer complete control over interior finishing. Utilities are connected to the plot line. The community has a clubhouse, jogging trails, a mosque, a small commercial area and round-the-clock security with a smart-gate entry system.

## Payment plan

Ten percent down payment with instalments over nine years. Finishing packages can be arranged through the developer at fixed prices.

## Notes

One of the last twin villas in this phase, with a southern garden orientation.
