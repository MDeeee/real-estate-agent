## Location

Red Sea Terraces is built on a gentle slope in Ain Sokhna with direct views of the Gulf of Suez. Cairo is about ninety minutes away by the Ain Sokhna Road, which makes weekend trips practical. The Galala City development, the Galala University and several hotels and restaurants are nearby, and the Sokhna Port is a short drive.

## The unit

A second-floor chalet of 105 m² with two bedrooms, two bathrooms, an open living and dining area and a large terrace facing the sea. Because of the terraced design, no building blocks the view. A small storage room and a laundry corner are included.

## Finishing and amenities

Delivered semi finished: plastering, plumbing and electrical rough-in complete, with the choice of floor and kitchen left to the buyer. The resort has a private beach with sun beds, two pools, a kids club, a gym, a market and several restaurants. Security is at the gate and on the beachfront, and parking is free within the complex.

## Payment plan

Ten percent down payment and eight years of instalments. A discount applies to an early final payment.

## Notes

A popular weekend destination, with year-round water sports and diving trips available from the resort.
