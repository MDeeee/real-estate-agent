## Location

Palm Crest sits in the heart of the Fifth Settlement, a few minutes from the Ninety Street and the Suez Road. The American University in Cairo and Cairo Festival City are both within a ten-minute drive, and Cairo International Airport is about twenty minutes away off-peak.

## The unit

A ground-floor apartment of 185 m² with a private 60 m² garden facing the central landscaped lake. Three bedrooms, each with its own bathroom, a wide reception area, a separate dining space and a closed kitchen with a laundry corner. Floor-to-ceiling windows bring in light from the garden side.

## Finishing and amenities

Delivered fully finished with porcelain flooring, built-in wardrobes in the master suite and split air-conditioning ducts in every room. Residents share a clubhouse, a heated swimming pool, a gym, a play area for children and a 24-hour gated security team. Underground parking is included with one reserved space.

## Payment plan

Fifteen percent down payment with the balance spread over six years, interest-free. A maintenance deposit is due at handover.
