## Location

Coral Shore is a mid-size resort in Sidi Heneish, a short drive from Hacienda Bay and Marina. Ras El Hekma road is a few minutes away, and the village is about two and a half hours from Cairo. A supermarket, pharmacy and clinic are inside the resort.

## The unit

A first-floor chalet of 110 m² with two bedrooms, two bathrooms, an open living and dining space and a terrace overlooking the largest of the resort swimming pools. The beach is a five-minute walk or a quick shuttle ride from the door. Bright interior with a neutral palette and quality bedding.

## Furnishing and services

Fully furnished and equipped for a family stay: air conditioning, a washing machine, a dishwasher, Wi-Fi and a smart TV. Access to the beach club, the pools, the gym and the kids club is included in the rental price. Daily cleaning can be arranged at an additional charge.

## Rental terms

Monthly rent of 65,000 EGP in season with a minimum stay of two months and a 25 percent deposit. Weekly rates are available in shoulder months.

## Notes

Cars are parked outside the main gate area. Early booking is advised for July and August.
